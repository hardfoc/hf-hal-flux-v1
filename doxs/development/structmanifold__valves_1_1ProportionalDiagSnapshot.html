Detailed Description

Per-channel TLE92466ED diagnostic snapshot.

Surfaces every per-channel fault and warning the driver IC reports plus the live current / duty / VBAT measurements:

  • overcurrent / short_to_ground / open_load / over_temperature: hard faults.
  • open_load_short_ground: combined OL or SG indication on the same output (some failure modes can't distinguish the two).
  • *_warning: pre-fault warnings (the chip flagged a precursor but didn't trip the fault).
  • current_regulation_warning / pwm_regulation_warning: control loop can't track setpoint — slew-rate, supply or load dynamics too aggressive.
  • average_current_raw / duty_cycle_raw: live measurement of what the channel is actually delivering. Can be cross-checked against the commanded setpoint as an additional safety layer.
