Detailed Description

Datasheet-driven solenoid model record.

Carries everything the SolenoidValve needs to push into the MAX22200:

  • Hit / hold currents (BinaryHitHold) computed from the rated voltage and coil resistance per the datasheet's Hit-and-Hold table.
  • Full-flow current (VariableCurrent) — the maximum current the proportional load is rated for at the chosen supply voltage. The SolenoidValve linearly maps position 0..1 → 0..full_flow_ma.
  • Per-channel diagnostic feature gates.
