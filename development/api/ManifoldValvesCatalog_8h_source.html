401// ─── PWM frequency note ───────────────────────────────────────────────────
402// The TLE92466ED datasheet caps the channel PWM frequency at 4 kHz (period
403// 250 µs) in normal mode (110 Hz – 4 kHz) or 500 Hz in low-frequency mode.
404// Anything we put here is clamped/validated by the driver; values above
405// 4 kHz will log a warning and be coerced down to spec. We therefore set
406// every model's `pwm_hz` to ≤ 4 kHz even when the valve datasheet allows
407// higher PWM — the chip is the binding constraint. See:
408// `hf-tle92466ed-driver/inc/tle92466ed_registers.hpp` (PERIOD::kSpecMax*).
409// ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
