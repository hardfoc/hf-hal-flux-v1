Pins that are inputs on Flux V1: the open-drain fault outputs of the two valve drivers.

GpioManager creates them as inputs from the first instruction, so the MCU never drives a chip's FAULT_N line, not even for the moment between pin creation and the valve manager's bring-up.

◆ kValveEnablePins

constexpr HfFunctionalGpioPin flux::board::kValveEnablePins[]
inlineconstexpr
Initial value:
= {
HfFunctionalGpioPin::MAX22200_ENABLE,
HfFunctionalGpioPin::TLE92466_EN,
}

The valve-bank enable outputs (MAX22200 ENABLE, TLE92466ED EN), active high.

Driving either low de-energises its whole bank in hardware, without SPI. GpioManager creates them driven inactive (low), so both banks are off until the valve manager's bring-up raises them; ValveManager::DropEnablePins() drives them low again.
